# KDWB audio sources

- `kdwb-office-studio-loop-v1.wav` is derived from **Busy Office No People Loop** by Fupicat on Freesound (CC0 1.0): https://freesound.org/people/Fupicat/sounds/534123/
- The six `kdwb-sfx-*` files were generated specifically for this project and are released as CC0 1.0.

All files are mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV and volume-balanced for phone playback.
